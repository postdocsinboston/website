## Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 14, 2026

The Postdocs in Boston website is maintained by volunteers associated with the Postdocs in Boston community, an informal community that is not a corporation, nonprofit, or other legal entity. References in this policy to “we,” “us,” or “our” mean the volunteer maintainers, as applicable. This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit [the website](https://postdocsinboston.github.io/website/) or contact its volunteer maintainers.

### Cookies and tracking

We do not use cookies or similar technologies for advertising or to track visitors across websites. GitHub Pages, which hosts this website, may process technical data as described below.

### Information handled by our hosting provider

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. The volunteer maintainers do not control GitHub’s hosting logs. See GitHub’s [Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement) and [GitHub Pages data collection information](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection).

### Information you send

If you use the contact form, the information you enter—which may include your name, email address, and message—may be received by the volunteer or volunteers who handle the form. They use it to respond to your inquiry or route it to the relevant volunteer. Please do not send confidential or sensitive information through the form.

The form is processed by **FORM SERVICE PROVIDER**. That provider may handle and retain information according to its own privacy policy. Messages are retained for **RETENTION PERIOD OR CRITERIA** and then deleted, unless retention is needed for an ongoing matter or required by law.

### External links and services

The website links to external services, including social media, WhatsApp, and mailing-list services. If you follow those links or contact the community through them, those services may collect information under their own privacy policies. The volunteer maintainers do not control those services or their data practices.

### How information is used and shared

Information sent to the volunteer maintainers may be used to respond to you, coordinate community activities, or maintain the website. The volunteer maintainers do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with volunteers or service providers who need it to handle your request or operate the website, or when required by law.

### Your choices and requests

You can contact the volunteer maintainers at **PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL** to ask about information you sent or request that it be corrected or deleted. Some information may need to be retained for an ongoing matter or where required by law. A service provider may also retain information under its own policies.

### Changes and contact

This policy may be updated by posting a revised version on this page. Questions or requests can be sent to **PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL**.
