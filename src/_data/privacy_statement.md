## Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 14, 2026

Postdocs in Boston (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is a volunteer-led community for postdoctoral researchers. This policy explains how information is handled when you visit [our website](https://postdocsinboston.github.io/website/) or contact us through it.

### Cookies and tracking

We do not use cookies or similar technologies for advertising or to track visitors across websites. GitHub Pages, which hosts this website, may use technical data as described below.

### Information handled by our hosting provider

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. We do not control GitHub's hosting logs. See GitHub's [Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement) and [GitHub Pages data collection information](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection).

### Information you send us

If you use the contact form, we receive the information you enter, which may include your name, email address, selected recipient, and message. We use it to respond to your inquiry and route it to the relevant volunteer or team member.

The form is processed by [service/provider]. That provider may handle and retain the information according to its own privacy policy. We retain messages for [period or criteria] and then delete them, unless we need to keep them for an ongoing matter or legal obligation.

### External links and services

The site links to external services, including social media, WhatsApp, and mailing-list services. If you follow those links or contact us through them, those services may collect information under their own privacy policies. We do not control their data practices.

### How we use and share information

We use information you send us to respond to you, coordinate community activities, and maintain the website. We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the volunteers or service providers who need it for those purposes, or when required by law.

### Your choices and requests

You can contact us at [privacy/contact email] to ask about information you have sent us or request that we correct or delete it. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or needed to address an ongoing matter.

### Changes and contact

We may update this policy by posting a revised version on this page. Questions or requests can be sent to [privacy/contact email].
