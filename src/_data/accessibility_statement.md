# Accessibility Statement

**Last updated:** October 14, 2026

The Postdocs in Boston website is maintained by volunteers associated with the Postdocs in Boston community, an informal community that is not a corporation, nonprofit, or other legal entity. We want the website to be usable by as many people as possible.

## Accessibility efforts

We are working to make the website easier to use and to improve its accessibility over time. **We have not completed a comprehensive accessibility audit.** We do not claim that every page or feature currently meets a particular accessibility standard.

## Report an accessibility issue

If you have difficulty using the website or encounter an accessibility barrier, contact the volunteer maintainers at ACCESSIBILITY CONTACT EMAIL. If possible, describe the page and the issue, and tell us how you would prefer to receive a response. We will consider your report and try to address the issue or provide the information in another way.

## Third-party content

The website may link to third-party websites or services. The volunteer maintainers do not control their accessibility or content. Please contact the relevant provider about accessibility issues on its service.

## Updates

We may update this statement as the website and our accessibility efforts change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
